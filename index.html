Moon phase

Many anglers treat new and full moons as stronger feeding windows, especially at first and last light. Use it as a tie-breaker, not a rule.

Barometric pressure

Bass often feed more actively when pressure is falling (ahead of a front) and tighten to cover when it rises sharply after a front.
